Approach

How I design for scale

Cost-gated pipelines

Free checks (titles, DNS, redirects, history in Supabase) run before paid ones, so scraping, enrichment and AI only touch records that earned it.

Rules first, AI for the grey zone

Deterministic rules settle the clear cases. AI gets the ambiguous ones with a structured evidence bundle and a strict output schema, and every call is logged.

One source of truth

Lead history lives in one Postgres database that every list, campaign and agent checks against, instead of being scattered across sheets.

Modular and re-runnable

Each stage is its own module with its own inputs and outputs, so a source, a rule set or a destination can change without rewriting the rest.
